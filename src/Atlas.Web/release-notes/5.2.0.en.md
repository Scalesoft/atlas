*25 November 2026*

### Highlights
Content you can trust: for every page you know who stands behind it and until when it holds, and in a space you see what people really read.
- **Verified content:** a page has an owner and a date until which it holds. The chip at its title shows whether it is verified, expiring soon or no longer valid; any editor verifies it with one click or the V key.
- **Content health:** in the space settings you see what is verified, what expires soon and what has no owner, and how many people read each page. What is read and needs verifying comes first.
- **Page discussion:** a colleague's new comment shows for a moment under the avatars. Reply right under it, or open the whole discussion in the panel, the same as on a board.
- **My colour:** in your profile you choose the colour others see you in, with your caret and avatar. Two people at one page or board always have different ones.
- **Reminders for owners:** 14 days before a page expires and on the day it does, its owner gets a notification. A reader can report a page as outdated.
- **AI knows what holds:** the assistant and the MCP tools know a page's verification and say so in their answer when it has expired.

### New
- **Page verification:** the chip at the title (Verified, Expires in 9 days, Verification expired, Not verified) opens the owner, who verified the page and when, and until when it holds. The published version is verified; „Verify until…“ picks a date of your own. When a newer version is published, the chip shows „Changed since verification“ with a comparison of the versions.
- **Owner:** a new page in a space with verification has its author as owner; change the owner in the chip or the page panel, and set owners in bulk in Content health by the author of the last published version.
- **Readership:** the page panel shows how many people read the page in 90 days. Content health shows views and distinct readers in 30, 90 or 365 days, never with names.
- **Verification in search:** search results and links to pages show their verification; the order of results does not change.
- **Quick reply in a discussion:** a new comment has a Reply button. Enter sends, Shift+Enter writes another line, Escape folds the reply. Alt C opens the page's discussion.
- **Collaborator colours:** one palette of twelve colours for carets, avatars and board pointers, in the light and dark look. Whoever comes to a page first keeps their colour.
- **Contact on a board:** a contact card fits its size, from the name alone to the whole card; the vCard and QR code are in the board toolbar, clear of the card's handle.
- **Board arrows:** drag an arrow's end onto another object, by touch too; the others see the line while you drag. From the keyboard: L on a selected arrow moves its end, Shift+L its start.
- **Sending a comment:** the same everywhere, on a page, a board or an object: Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, or the paper-plane button.

### Improvements
- **Updated libraries:** the editor (Tiptap 3.35, collaboration y-tiptap 3.3.1 without a patch of our own), the database driver Npgsql 11, Wolverine 6.65, Vue 3.5 and more.

### Administration
- A space administrator turns verification on in the General settings with an interval of 3, 6 or 12 months. A page can have an interval of its own or none. Until it is on, a space looks as in 5.1.
- New MCP tools and `/api/v1` endpoints for page verification, content health and reporting an outdated page. A verification through MCP or the API is marked „via AI“ on the page.
