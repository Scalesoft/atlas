### Highlights
You can see where the others are writing on the page.
- **Co-editor pointers:** while editing a page together, you see the others' pointers and selections live, each in their own colour and with their name.
- **Who knows what:** clicking a person also shows what they know (topics as tags with a level of 1–5); rate yourself or a colleague, and find in Contacts who knows a topic. The same topic spelled differently joins by itself, similar ones merge with one button. Only a wiki admin sees who gave what. Contacts show a topic's overview (levels, related topics, pages) and a who–what graph. You can also ask the AI: "who did informed consent in the Orion project?"
