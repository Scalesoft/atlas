### Highlights
Together on one page: you see where everyone is and what they write, and who knows what.
- **Co-editor pointers:** while editing a page or a board together, you see live where the others are and what they write, each in their own colour and with their name.
- **AI writes before your eyes:** when the assistant or an agent writes into an open page through MCP or the API, the text appears step by step and its caret shows where.
- **Business cards with a QR code:** hover over a person to see their card; download the contact card as a .vcf, or scan its QR code with your phone, even without access to the wiki.
- **Knowledge map:** who knows what, with ratings from colleagues, a topic overview and a graph of connections; just type “Who knows…” into the search in Contacts.
- **A livelier page history:** the chart on top shows who wrote how much and when, AI and publications included.
- **PostgreSQL 19:** Atlas runs on the new database version from the official image; it converts a PostgreSQL 18 database by itself on the first start.

### New
- **Co-editor pointers:** on a page or a board you see the mouse and caret of everyone who is there right now, in their colour and with their name, moving smoothly.
- **AI writing live:** a change from the assistant or an agent through MCP or the API is typed into an open page word by word, and the AI caret stays by it for a moment. The AI avatar among the co-editors shows it is at work. Your own typing is kept meanwhile.
- **Person card:** hover over a person in the text (tap on a phone) to see their photo, role, company, phones, e-mails, Write an e-mail and Call buttons, and what they know.
- **Contact card for your phone:** from the person card and from the directory card, download the contact card as a .vcf or unfold its QR code. The whole card is in the code, so a phone saves it even outside the company network.
- **Knowledge map:** every person has the topics they know, with a level of 1–5. Rate yourself or a colleague privately (only you see it), anonymously or under your name. Add a topic quickly from the person card, or to several people at once.
- **Who knows…:** in Contacts, a topic lists its people by level, related and broader topics (C# belongs to .NET, say), its pages and a who–what graph. You can ask the AI the same, e.g. “who did informed consent in the Orion project?”.
- **Tidy topics:** a topic spelled differently joins by itself, similar topics merge with one button. A topic someone else gave you can be removed by you or a wiki admin.
- **Page history:** the chart on top shows each person's lines in the colour of their caret, AI apart and publications as marks; the version under the pointer lights up in the chart. A change through MCP or the API names its client, and every version lists its contributors with their share.
- **What's new screen** after an update shows the release's highlights with live demos.

### Improvements
- Anonymous ratings stay anonymous: counts show only in bands (“at least 3”) and the average does not move in a way that gives a new rating away. Who gave whom what is visible only to a wiki admin in the app.
- Search keeps an exact name match (a contact, say) on top while further results arrive; contacts list an exact match first, then a name that starts with the words, diacritics or not.
- Dialogs, the search included, close on a click outside them, just like Escape.
- With many co-editors on one page the editor stays snappy: the others' mouse moves no longer redraw the whole text.
- A person in the text has the same colour as their caret and avatar.
- A history with many versions draws within a second, and its chart follows the light and dark look.

### Fixes
- A co-editor who closes the page leaves it at once; avatars are no longer duplicated. After a short connection drop the others see you again at once.
- The connection card and a link's tools no longer cover the text or the mode switch.
- Several agent writes in a row show one AI avatar, not several.

### Administration and operations
- The database runs on PostgreSQL 19 from the official `pgvector/pgvector` image; Atlas's own database image is gone.
- The PostgreSQL 18 database is converted by itself on the first start of 5.1 (`docker compose up -d` with the new `docker-compose.yml`). The old data stay untouched: going back to 5.0 is the previous `docker-compose.yml`. The details are in the operations documentation.
- When the database's text sort version changes, Atlas rebuilds the indexes once at startup.
- Only a wiki admin can change which account a contact belongs to.
