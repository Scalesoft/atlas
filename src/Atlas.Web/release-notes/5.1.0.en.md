### Highlights
Together on one page: you see who is writing where, and Atlas knows who knows what.
- **Co-editor pointers:** while editing a page or a board together, you see live where the others are and what they write, each in their own colour and with their name.
- **AI caret:** where the assistant or an agent writes through MCP or the API, its caret shows for a moment.
- **Business cards with a QR code:** hover over a person to see their card; download the contact card as a .vcf, or scan its QR code with your phone.
- **Knowledge map:** who knows what, with ratings, a topic overview and a graph of connections; just ask “Who knows…”.
- **PostgreSQL 19:** Atlas runs on the new database version; a ready script moves a PostgreSQL 18 database and keeps the original for a rollback.

### New
- Hover over a person in the text to see their card: photo, role, company, phones, e-mails and Send e-mail and Call buttons. On a phone, a tap opens it. From the card you download the contact card as a .vcf, or unfold its QR code and scan it with a phone. The whole card is inside the code, so the phone saves it without a connection to the wiki. The directory's contact card offers the same.
- Knowledge map: clicking a person also shows what they know (topics as tags with a level of 1–5); rate yourself or a colleague, and find in Contacts who knows a topic. The same topic spelled differently joins by itself, similar ones merge with one button. Only a wiki admin sees who gave what, and a topic others put on you is yours (or an admin's) to remove. Contacts show a topic's overview (levels, related topics, pages) and a who–what graph. You can also ask the AI: "who did informed consent in the Orion project?"
- Page history is livelier: the chart on top shows each person's lines in the colour of their caret, AI apart and publications as marks; the version under the pointer or selected lights up in the chart. A version's contributors wear their colour, a change through MCP or the API also names its client (the token's name, for those allowed to see it), steps come in one after another and the version time shows in full in English.

### Fixes
- A co-editor who closes the page leaves it at once; co-editor avatars are no longer duplicated. After a short connection drop the others see you again at once.
- Search keeps an exact name match (a contact, say) on top while further results arrive.
- Dialogs, the search included, close on a click outside them, just like Escape.
