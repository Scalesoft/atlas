### Highlights
Together on one page: you see who is writing where, and Atlas knows who knows what.
- **Co-editor pointers:** while editing a page or a board together, you see live where the others are and what they write, each in their own colour and with their name.
- **AI caret:** where the assistant or an agent writes through MCP or the API, its caret shows for a moment.
- **Business cards with a QR code:** hover over a person to see their card; download the contact card as a .vcf, or scan its QR code with your phone.
- **Knowledge map:** who knows what, with ratings, a topic overview and a graph of connections; just ask “Who knows…”.
- **PostgreSQL 19:** Atlas runs on the new database version; a ready script moves a PostgreSQL 18 database and keeps the original for a rollback.

### New
- Hover over a person in the text to see their card: photo, role, company, phones, e-mails and Send e-mail and Call buttons. On a phone, a tap opens it. From the card you download the contact card as a .vcf, or unfold its QR code and scan it with a phone. The whole card is inside the code, so the phone saves it without a connection to the wiki. The directory's contact card offers the same.
