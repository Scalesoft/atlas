*14 November 2026*

### Highlights
The access overview says who may go where and why, and a page's source is one switch away.
- **Access overview:** content is grouped by space with the spaces' real icons and colours; access to a whole space is separated from pages with their own access and from other resources.
- **Why this access:** each access shows where it comes from, through which group it applies and which level each source gives.
- **Hub verification:** the periodic verification of membership is shown apart from the real end of an access and no longer looks like an approaching loss of permission.
- **Document | Source** `Esc`: the switch stays in place, Source fills the rest of the window and the caret goes straight into it; Esc returns to the text.
- **API and MCP:** the "API access" setting has a new name, so the MCP permission is easy to find too.

### New
- A page with its own access now says that it does not inherit the regular space permissions. The details of an access can be expanded.
- The YouTrack integration has a clear name, labelled project identifiers and a working link to the integration overview.
- An unsaved source draft asks what to do with it before returning to the document and before locking the page.

### Improvements
- The active row when picking modules from the catalog has a continuous outline instead of an unclear bracket on the left edge.
- The account, tokens, groups and activity remain available under "API and MCP" as before.
- A page left open during the deployment of a new version reloads itself when nothing being written would be lost; otherwise the notice with its button stays.
- Frames and groups on a board in the blue and violet tones no longer show yellow dots on their edge.
- **Order pages** with older requirement blocks are converted to headings even when they have two lists right after each other or a page link in the text or name of a requirement. Until now they stayed in the fallback view.
